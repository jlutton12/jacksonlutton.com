About

Jackson Lutton

IT Support & Systems Technician · Dallas–Fort Worth, TX

I'm a helpdesk technician at an MSP in the Dallas–Fort Worth area, supporting clients across Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Intune and Autopilot device management, and networking. Day to day that means everything from onboarding a new hire's laptop to migrating an office off an aging on-prem server — and explaining what's happening in a way that leaves people feeling taken care of.

I graduated from the University of Oklahoma in 2025 with a B.S. in Information Science & Technology, and I'm currently studying for the CompTIA Network+. I run a Proxmox homelab at home to practice the same tools clients use — virtualization, DNS, reverse proxies, monitoring — in an environment where breaking things is the point.

I believe good IT support is as much about communication as it is about technical knowledge. When I'm not at a keyboard, you'll find me outdoors, on the water, or on the golf course.
